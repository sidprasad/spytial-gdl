01 / Order

Keep the words in order.

A Mermaid user drawing the grammatical structure of an Indonesian sentence needed its words to stay in sequence.

Here, a short English sentence illustrates the same requirement. Its words stay on one line, in reading order, while the phrases sit above their parts.

Adapted example, not a translation of the original sentence.

Original discussion: Mermaid #815 ↗

02 / Grouping

Keep partners together.

Adding another generation to a DOT family tree placed one partner between members of a different couple. Reordering the declarations did not give the author the arrangement they wanted.

In this smaller family, partners share a row and a group. Children stay below their parents. John and Jane share a row too.

A subset of the family in the discussion, with shorter labels. The boxes identify couples; arrows run from parent to child.

Original discussion: Graphviz ↗

03 / Orientation

Connect it without turning it.

A Mermaid user specified a left-to-right subgraph. Connecting one of its nodes to the outside changed its direction.

This compiler pipeline has its own left-to-right order. Source enters from above and a program leaves below, without changing that internal order.

An illustrative pipeline based on the reported layout problem, not the original graph. Mermaid documents this subgraph limitation.

Original discussion: Mermaid #4738 ↗
source[Source]:::Artifact -> parse[Parse]:::Stage : enters parse -> check[Check types]:::Stage : stage check -> generate[Generate code]:::Stage : stage generate -> program[Program]:::Artifact : leaves class parse,check,generate compiler @group(selector=compiler, name='Compiler') @orientation(selector=stage, directions=[right]) @align(selector=stage, direction=horizontal) @orientation(selector=enters, directions=[directlyBelow]) @orientation(selector=leaves, directions=[directlyBelow]) @edgeStyle(field=stage, showLabel=false) @edgeStyle(field=enters, showLabel=false) @edgeStyle(field=leaves, showLabel=false) @atomStyle(selector=univ, textStyle(size=large)) @atomStyle(selector=Stage, fillStyle(color='#1f4396'), textStyle(color=white)) @atomStyle(selector=Artifact, fillStyle(color='#edb211'), textStyle(color='#16130f'))

04 / Alignment

A connection is not a hierarchy.

A Mermaid user needed two connected nodes on the same level. Their cross-connections instead put them at different heights.

Here, two replicas exchange updates as peers. Their connection keeps them aligned, while each store sits below its replica.

The issue's five-node topology with illustrative system labels. This is a layout example, not a replication protocol. The original request notes DOT's rank=same alternative.

Original discussion: Mermaid #3723 ↗

About these examples

The discussions document their authors' needs at the time. Some include workarounds; they are not claims about what every current renderer can do. These are our adaptations, not endorsements by the original authors.

SPytial preserves the spatial requirements you write. It does not establish that the graph is a correct account of a sentence, family, or system.
